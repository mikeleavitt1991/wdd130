Calgary, Alberta, Canada

Calgary is a major city in the western Canadian province of Alberta, situated at the confluence of the Bow and Elbow Rivers near the foothills of the Canadian Rockies. Known for its high quality of life, thriving energy and technology sectors, and rich cultural heritage, Calgary serves as a key economic hub. It is home to the famous annual Calgary Stampede and offers world-class outdoor recreation with Banff National Park located just a short drive to the west.
